## Spoken version

Your cheese was seventy two dollars a case. Now it’s seventy eight. We flag the increase.
One vendor charges seventy eight. The other charges seventy five. We line up the same cheese, even when the item numbers are different.
That increase adds ten cents to your cheese pizza. Your food cost is now thirty point six percent. Your target is thirty. That’s the item to check.
The vendor question is already written. Alex and the due time are filled in. Review it. Change something only if you need to.
Approve the follow up. You don’t have to start from a blank form. In this preview, nothing is sent.
Keep your food costs in sight. Know what changed, see the menu impact, and review the next step. Try your Never86 owner seat.

## UI wording decisions

* Owner seat replaces desk.
* Alex and Riley are people. Remove the sample manager suffix.
* What went up replaces price drift in the main explanation.
* Same cheese and box label replace SKU matching language.
* Percent left after prep replaces usable yield.
* Review the prepared follow up replaces ask what changed, name an owner, keep the reply.
* One approval is the primary action. Person and due time are already entered. Overrides are optional.

## Restaurant logic

The fictional recipe sells for $16 before tax. It uses 8 oz of cheese and $3.60 of other ingredients. Cheese rises from $2.40 to $2.60 per usable pound. Ingredient cost goes from $4.80 to $4.90 per pizza. Food cost goes from 30% to 30.625%, displayed as 30.6%. The 30% target is editable and belongs to this example. It is not a recommended benchmark. This is ingredient cost, not net margin. Labor, rent, packaging, delivery charges and unrecorded waste are excluded.

The vendor comparison still checks case weight, recipe fit, delivery, stock and minimums. It does not change suppliers, menu prices or food cost rules on its own. Same product mapping in this public preview is predefined. It does not demonstrate general OCR or automatic matching of arbitrary uploaded invoices. Approval stays in the preview tab and sends nothing. Reload clears it.

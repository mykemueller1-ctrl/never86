## Production directions

Record a clean browser window with personal bookmarks, email, extensions and private tabs hidden. Use the public sample route only. Frame one record and one result at a time. Show the mouse movement and a real click before each result.

Create a 1920 × 1080 master. Make a 1080 × 1080 social version by reframing each step, not shrinking an entire desktop into a square. Export MP4 with H.264 video, AAC audio if narrated, and a 30 fps target. Keep the 60 second export under 100 MB as our production target. These are editing choices, not claims of an algorithm advantage.

Use the actual forest green and cream demo styling. Keep “SAMPLE DEMO” visible in each scene. Put captions above the bottom interface controls. Use short caption lines, large enough to read on a phone. Review “Never86’d,” units, dates and amounts manually. LinkedIn supports automatic captions and a review option. Scheduled video captions may only become available after posting. [LinkedIn caption guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1360638)

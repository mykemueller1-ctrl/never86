# Never 86'd — 100 Statement Audit Launch

## Campaign objective

Drive restaurant owners to submit one redacted DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub statement for a free marketplace-cost and payout-math audit.

Primary conversion event: completed form at `/audit`, followed by the operator replying to the intake email with a statement attached.

## Non-negotiable positioning

- Lead with the operator's money problem, not AI.
- Say **free audit**, not demo.
- Never promise a refund or recovery before the evidence proves one.
- Keep `identified`, `disputed`, `recovered`, and `protected` dollars separate.
- One statement creates the first proof. POS and bank records complete the full reconciliation.
- Public line: **Bring the file. Show the math. Keep the receipt.**

# Measurement

Track daily:

- video/post views;
- profile visits;
- link clicks;
- audit starts;
- completed statement uploads;
- audits completed;
- repeat operators;
- verified dollars disputed;
- verified dollars recovered or protected.

Never combine “identified,” “disputed,” and “recovered.” They are different states.

# Claim Guardrails

- Do not say the OpenAI app is approved until approval is documented.
- Do not say DoorDash owes a refund unless the audit and supporting records prove it.
- Do not call promotions or ads a leak without measuring whether they produced incremental contribution.
- Keep verified, calculated, and missing evidence visibly separate.
- Do not use customer data or audit screenshots publicly without permission.

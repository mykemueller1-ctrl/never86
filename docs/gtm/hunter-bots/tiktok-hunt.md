# TikTok hunt — listen in comments, don't spam FYP

**Hunter job:** find **operators** venting in search results and **comment threads**.  
**GTM job (later):** post proof videos — 515 / Never86 / Community Tap voices.

**Voice:** Myke peer in comments — not brand ad speak.

---

## Hunt routine (10 min)

### 1. TikTok Search (in app or Grok web)

Natural language — TikTok SEO indexes spoken words + captions:

```
DoorDash fees restaurant owner
restaurant profit margins 2026
third party delivery killing my restaurant
Uber Eats payout wrong restaurant
restaurant owner struggling margins
Grubhub commission food truck
MarginEdge restaurant
```

### 2. Hashtags to **browse** (not spam-post)

| Tag | Use |
|---|---|
| `#restaurantowner` | Operator identity posts |
| `#restaurantlife` | Day-in-life — read comments for owners |
| `#restauranttok` | Mixed — filter for owner voice |
| `#smallbusinessowner` | Indie vent threads |
| `#doordash` `#ubereats` | Fee rant videos — **read comments** |
| `#iowa` `#desmoines` `#midwest` | Geo when hunting local |

Use **3–5 tags** when posting (GTM only). Hunter uses **search**, not hashtag floods.

### 3. Comment-thread hunt (high yield)

On videos where someone rants about:
- delivery fees
- "closing my restaurant"
- payout doesn't match
- promo spend

**Read comments.** Operators reply as owners ("we raised menu prices on the app…"). Reply to **those** comments with peer help + one audit link.

### 4. Victor / Iowa lane

- Monitor **@ontheline515** comment sections for owners asking money questions
- Local operator videos mentioning Iowa cities
- Do not hijack Victor's voice — Myke or Never86 account replies separately if needed

## TikTok reply format (short)

TikTok comments are short. Max 2–3 sentences.

```
The commission on the contract isn't the whole bill — promos and marketing on the statement are usually worse. I run a spot in Iowa — free check on one redacted DoorDash/Uber statement if you want the math separated. Link in my bio or: never86.ai/audit
```

**Tracked link when posting from brand account:**

```
https://www.never86.ai/audit?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=hunter&utm_campaign=100_statement_audit&utm_content=COMMENT_[date]_1
```

On TikTok: often **link in bio** + say "AUDIT in comments" if no clickable link.

---

## What NOT to do on TikTok

- Post product demo FYP spam daily
- Copy-paste same comment 50 times (ban risk)
- Chase food bloggers or mukbang — not ICP
- Promise "we'll find you money"
- Lead with "AI" or "platform"

## Locked thesis

**The Reel is the storefront. Social creates demand. Bolt closes the loop.**

Restaurant content already creates appetite and intent. Do not make a guest leave the social experience, search for the restaurant again, compare marketplace listings, and surrender the relationship. Give the clip one direct order path:

`Reel → restaurant-owned checkout → Never86 Bolt order routing → restaurant + delivery → guest relationship stays with the operator`

Third-party marketplaces can remain controlled overflow where Bolt coverage is unavailable. They are not the default front door.

## Platform truth

- TikTok Destination Links can attach an approved CTA and external landing page to eligible organic videos, with verification and Pixel prerequisites.
- TikTok links can open in TikTok's in-app browser.
- Current United States TikTok Shop food rules cover shelf-stable products and prohibit homemade food and non-durable carry-out packaging. Do **not** claim native TikTok Shop checkout for hot restaurant meals.
- Facebook/Instagram supported ordering providers can add Order Food buttons that send guests to an ordering website.

The honest first release is a very fast mobile restaurant checkout opened from social with minimal context switch—not a fictional native-hot-food platform integration.

## Bounded beta spec Grok must produce

Use two restaurants and one orderable hero item per restaurant for 30 days.

1. Restaurant/business-account eligibility and order-link setup.
2. Clip-specific destination that lands on the exact featured item.
3. Guest checkout without forced account creation.
4. Pickup/delivery choice, payment, and plain delivery promise.
5. Exact accept, route, dispatch, pickup, exception, and delivered owners.
6. Restaurant/POS handoff described as a contract until a real integration is verified.
7. Consented first-party guest data, privacy controls, opt-out, and audit receipt.
8. Measurement from Reel view → CTA tap → menu load → checkout start → paid order → delivery → repeat order.
9. Beta scorecard based on completed orders, contribution margin, delivery exceptions, data capture, and repeat purchase—not views alone.
10. Stop conditions for unsupported market coverage, failed order routing, missing rights, unverified partner claims, or unclear operator economics.

# Never86'd 52-week 3P authority system

## What was built tonight

The public corpus contains 52 operator field guides. They are available as human-readable HTML, a concise `llms.txt` index, a full-text `llms-full.txt` corpus, a JSON answer API, an Atom feed, and an XML sitemap. A separate public research brief documents the August 14-20 signal sample and its limits.

The 52 pages are the starting inventory, not a license to manufacture 52 claims. Each week upgrades one guide with fresh primary-source verification, a permitted operator example, or a reproducible calculation.

## Weekly operating loop

1. **Verify** — reopen the marketplace or industry source; record any changed policy, field, report, or date.
2. **Prove** — add one redacted, permissioned operator example or label the week as a method-only update when no case exists.
3. **Publish** — update the guide, source-check date, sitemap date, feed, and correction note when the conclusion changes.
4. **Explain** — create one podcast segment or short operator explanation using the guide's real question and evidence boundary.
5. **Distribute** — share the answer where it genuinely helps. No duplicate comments, cold-DM spray, or invented operator voice.
6. **Measure** — run the neutral prompt set in `config/llm-discovery-prompts.json` and record mentions, citations, cited URLs, answer accuracy, and referral traffic.
7. **Learn** — route new language, missing records, and repeated questions back into the audit workflow and next guide update.

## The weekly scorecard

- Index coverage: public guides discovered / 52.
- Citation share: neutral prompts with a Never86'd citation / 30.
- Accurate citation share: citations that preserve the product and evidence boundaries / all Never86'd citations.
- Source freshness: guides checked in the last 90 days / 52.
- Third-party authority: new independent operator, accountant, podcast, publication, or partner references that link to a relevant guide.
- Qualified behavior: statement-audit starts and completed redacted submissions from answer-engine or organic referrals.
- Proof behavior: operators who return with a second finalized period without being chased.
- Corrections: material public corrections, time to repair, and whether the change was disclosed.

## Guardrails

- No fake Reddit, Quora, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, review, or forum accounts.
- No purchased links, link farms, doorway pages, spun articles, or duplicated city/platform pages.
- No copied posts or testimonials without permission.
- No invented savings, recovered-cash, prevalence, market-size, or contract-violation claims.
- No marketplace portal credentials and no public restaurant-private data.
- No implication that DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, ezCater, or another platform endorses Never86'd.
- Keep observed cost, cash reconciliation, contract compliance, and operating diagnosis as separate claims.
- A week with no new evidence can still improve a guide's explanation, redaction instructions, calculation, or stated limit.

## First 12 distribution themes

1. One redacted statement before integration.
2. Estimated transactions versus finalized payouts.
3. Monthly statement versus deposit timing.
4. Commission versus all-in observed marketplace cost.
5. Restaurant-funded versus platform-funded promotions.
6. Error charges: operational pattern versus disputable case.
7. Why POS sales and platform statements differ.
8. How to match a payout with the bank without forcing a tie.
9. Why net-deposit accounting hides the money path.
10. The first 30 minutes of a payout investigation.
11. Small-operator controls that scale to multi-unit groups.
12. What Never86'd has and has not proven.

After week 12, continue in field-guide order. Prefer depth, corrections, and real operator evidence over publishing volume.

# Earned authority outreach pack

## The objective

Earn one independent, context-rich reference to a relevant Never86'd guide each week. A useful reference is a restaurant, accountant, podcast, publication, consultant, or technology partner explaining why the guide helped them understand a real operator question. It is not a purchased link or a pasted marketing slogan.

## Permissioned operator request

Subject: Can we document the method without naming your restaurant?

> We are improving a public guide for restaurant operators reviewing delivery-marketplace statements. May we use a redacted version of the question, records supplied, calculation method, and outcome? We will remove guest, bank, tax, credential, and unnecessary restaurant identifiers. You will review the exact case text before publication. We will not call a charge wrong, recoverable, or contract-noncompliant unless the required evidence supports that conclusion.

## Podcast or publication pitch

Subject: The delivery commission is not the whole restaurant cost

> Myke Mueller is an active restaurant operator building a public evidence desk for DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub reconciliation. The useful story is not that every platform charge is wrong. It is that small operators often have no time to connect the statement, payout, promotion funding, refunds, and bank deposit. We can bring a redacted, reproducible example, explain what the evidence proves, and show where it stops. The public method and source links are available at https://www.never86.ai/delivery-marketplace-reconciliation.

## Accountant or consultant reference request

> We published a restaurant-friendly month-end workflow that keeps observed marketplace cost, cash receipt, contract compliance, and operating diagnosis separate. If it is useful to your clients, would you review it for clarity and link to the exact guide you would give an operator? Please point out anything that should be corrected before referencing it.

## Human-reviewed social response rule

Reply only when a public operator has asked a question the guide directly answers. Disclose the Never86'd relationship, answer the question in the comment itself, link to one relevant guide at most, and do not repeat the same comment across threads. Never automate publishing, impersonate an operator, manufacture agreement, or pressure someone to leave a testimonial.

## Weekly distribution checklist

1. One permissioned proof or source-verified method update.
2. One full podcast transcript, guest article, or partner resource when available.
3. Up to three human-reviewed public answers where the link is directly useful.
4. One request for an independent technical or accounting review.
5. Search Console, Bing, IndexNow, neutral-prompt, citation, and referral results recorded before choosing the next guide.
